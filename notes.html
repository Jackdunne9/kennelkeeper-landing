Behind the build

Assigned case: H — Waggy Tails Dog Daycare, Lucan.

Original hunch (written after AI interaction)

Busy people looking for somewhere to look after their dog hassle free whilst they go on holidays

Preserved verbatim. This sentence was supplied after AI was already opened and therefore is not represented as a pre-AI hunch.

Chosen niche

Independent dog boarding kennels in Ireland. Buyer: kennel owner/manager. Everyday user: kennel staff. Invented buyer quote: “I need one place to see who's booked in and which vaccinations need checking.” Invented staff quote: “I don't have time to type long updates while caring for the dogs.”

Technology: browser-based booking, vaccination and activity tracker, with a basic owner-view prototype. Current approach (assumption): messages, diaries, paper vaccination records and manual owner updates.

Existing services

KennelBooker — features and Pet Sitter Plus — dog boarding software. These are relevant competitor references; feature details and source-check dates should be independently verified before submission. Links included 19 October 2026.

Business model (assumption)

€99 one-time setup plus €39/month per kennel. This assumes a small business values predictable costs and onboarding assistance. No customers or revenue are claimed.

Lean PRD (five lines)

Hypothesis: Independent kennel owners will express interest in a simple capacity-and-vaccination tracker.
Audience: Owners/managers of small independent Irish dog boarding kennels.
Single CTA: Join the simulated waiting list.
Success measure: In a real future test, measure visitor-to-signup conversion; the current prototype does not collect signups.
Out of scope: Payments, real messaging, multi-device sync, secure customer authentication and real vaccination verification.

Implementation assumptions

Demo dates use the browser's local date; each sample dog counts as one place. Vaccination is represented by one kennel-cough expiry date. An expired date blocks check-in and booking; a date within 14 days displays amber. Owner codes are illustrative, not real authentication. All sample changes are stored only in the browser's localStorage.
